# Active Curl Finger Strength Repaters (AC FSR)

- Test Active Curl Peak Load (MVC)
- 1 set of 8 reps at 60% L/R - warmup (2 seconds contraction, 4 seconds rest), Rest 3 - 5 min.
  In short notation: 8x AC @20mm 60%MVC 2:4s:3min  
- 1 set of 5 reps at 70% L/R - warmup (2 seconds contraction, 4 seconds rest), Rest 3 - 5 min.
  In short notation: 5x AC @20mm 70%MVC 2:4s:3min  
- 5 sets of 5 reps at 80% L/R - main (2 seconds contraction, 4 seconds rest), Rest 3 - 5 min. 
  In short notation: 5 sets 5x AC @20mm 80%MVC 2:4s:3min  
- Progress volume over 4 - 6 weeks to reach 10 sets of 5 reps at 80% MVC
- Retest Active Curl Peak Load, adjust the settings and continue with the program
